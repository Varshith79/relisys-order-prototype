1Before you start

  • Use a laptop or desktop with Chrome, Edge or Safari for the full view. You will see an Android phone and an iPhone side by side. They show the same app, because Relisys will have one app for both kinds of phone.
  • Or use your own mobile phone. Open the mobile link to try the app full screen, the way a customer would.
  • You don't need to install, sign up or enter a password. Nothing you do here is sent to SAP or to any customer.
  • The data: products, item codes, order numbers, delivery challans (DCs), invoices, dates and quantities come from Relisys SAP Business One. Customer and staff names, codes, mobile numbers, emails, GSTINs and addresses are sample data, used to protect real customers' details.

2How the prototype page works

On a laptop, the page has three parts:

PartWhat it does
Shortcut buttons at the top (Place an order, Other tabs, Relisys staff)Jump straight to any screen. Use them if you get lost or want to look at one screen again.

Part AA customer places an order

In this part you are a hospital or distributor buying from Relisys. The sample customer used is Harmony Hospital Pvt Ltd (C1105).

Step 1 · Sign in with a mobile number

  1. On the first screen, find the box Demo: sign in as a customer.
  2. Leave Harmony Hospital Pvt Ltd (C1105) selected and tap Use this customer.
  3. The screen asks for a 6-digit code. Type any 6 digits (for example 123456).
  4. Tap Verify & sign in.

In the real app: the customer types their mobile number and Relisys sends a one-time code by SMS. Only mobile numbers saved on the customer in SAP can sign in, so there are no usernames or passwords to remember.

Step 2 · The Products screen (home)

Look at these parts from top to bottom:

  • Ordering for · C1105: the company you are signed in as.
  • Bell icon (top right): notifications, with the number of new ones.
  • Search box: type a size, item code or product name, for example 3.00 x 24, PRP-30024 or Pristine.
  • Last order card with a Reorder button that puts the whole last order in the cart again.
  • Your products: the products this customer has ordered before.
  • All products: every product group from SAP. Switch between Relisys products and OEM.

Note: prices and stock are not shown, by design. Relisys applies the agreed prices on the invoice.

Step 3 · Choose sizes and quantities

  1. Tap Premier+.
  2. Under 1 · Diameter (mm), tap a diameter, for example 3.00.
  3. Under 2 · Length & quantity, tap + next to two or three lengths. Tap + again to raise a quantity, − to lower it.
  4. Change the diameter and add one more size. The sizes you already added stay in the cart.

Look for: lines like “10 already pending on open orders”. They warn the customer that this size is already ordered and not yet shipped, so they don't order it twice. The bar at the bottom counts sizes and units in the cart.

  1. Tap View cart.

Step 4 · Cart and ship-to address

  1. Check the sizes and quantities. Change them with + and −.
  2. Under Ship to, pick one of the ship-to addresses. They come from the customer's addresses in SAP (this customer has 14).
  3. Tap Place order.

In the real app: the order goes straight into SAP as a Sales Order for this customer and ship-to address.

Step 5 · My orders

After placing the order, the app opens My orders.

  1. Your new order is at the top with status Placed and the note “Waiting for Relisys to set the delivery date”.
  2. Below it are this customer's orders from the last 90 days, from SAP.
  3. Tap the filters at the top: Placed, Under process, Partly shipped, Shipped. Then tap All again.
  4. Optional: tap Cancel order on your new order. A customer can cancel only until Relisys sets the delivery date.

Step 6 · Order details: shipments and invoices

  1. In My orders, tap Order 271001032 (status Partly shipped).
  2. Read the timeline: Placed → Under process (delivery date set) → each shipment with its DC number, date and units → each invoice number.
  3. Tap Pending to see what is still to ship, and Shipped to see what has gone.
  4. Reorder these 20 sizes puts the same sizes in the cart again.
  5. Tap the back arrow (top left) to return.

This answers the customer's most common phone call, “where is my order?”, without them having to call Relisys.

Step 7 · Frequent items

  1. Tap Frequent items in the bottom bar.
  2. You see the sizes this customer orders most often, how many orders they appeared in, and the usual quantity.
  3. Tap + once: it adds the customer's usual quantity in one tap.
  4. Tap the Save icon next to a size, then open the Saved tab: the customer's own list of sizes to keep handy.

Step 8 · Notifications

  1. Go to Products and tap the bell icon at the top right.
  2. You see messages such as “Order received”, “Order … is under process” and “Order … partly shipped · DC … units shipped”.
  3. Tap Mark all read.

In the real app: these also arrive as phone notifications and, where wanted, SMS.

Step 9 · Account

  1. Tap Account in the bottom bar.
  2. You see the company details from SAP: login mobile (partly hidden), contact, email, GSTIN, billing address and all ship-to addresses.
  3. Tap Show all 14 ship-to addresses.

Customers can't edit these details in the app. Changes are made by Relisys in SAP, so SAP stays the single source of truth.

Step 10 · Help & contact, and sign out

  1. In Account, tap Help & contact Relisys.
  2. Open the common questions, for example “What do the order statuses mean?”.
  3. Go back to Account and tap Sign out. You are back at the sign-in screen.

Part BRelisys staff screens

Staff use the same app. They sign in with their SAP Business One username and password and get extra screens. Staff can look at the app as any customer sees it, but they don't place orders for customers.

Step 11 · Staff sign in

  1. On the sign-in screen, tap Relisys staff? Sign in with SAP at the bottom.
  2. In the box Demo: sign in as a staff user, leave Relisys Admin (manager) selected.
  3. Tap Use this SAP user.

In the real app: SAP checks the username and password. The app never stores the password. Only SAP users given access in Step 17 can sign in, and a user locked in SAP loses access straight away.

Step 12 · Customers list

  1. You see every customer that has a Mobile Phone in SAP, busiest first, with their number of orders and whether they can sign in.
  2. Type part of a name or code in the search box.
  3. Try the filters: Ordered in 90 days, Products hidden, Blocked, then All.
  4. Tap Harmony Hospital Pvt Ltd. A panel opens with its mobile, contact, orders and the products it sees.

Step 13 · Open as customer (view only)

  1. In the panel, tap Open as customer.
  2. A dark bar at the top says “Staff view · view only”. Below it is exactly what this customer sees.
  3. Open a product and tap +. The app shows “View only. Customers place their own orders.”
  4. Open My orders to see the customer's orders as they see them. This helps when a customer calls with a question.
  5. Tap Exit in the dark bar to return to the staff screens.

Step 14 · Choose the products a customer sees

  1. Tap Products shown in the bottom bar (or Choose products shown in a customer's panel).
  2. Pick a customer in the Customer list.
  3. Tap the OEM-FG filter and switch off one or two product groups. Their line changes to “Hidden from customer”, and the counts at the top update.
  4. Tap Open as customer: the hidden groups are gone from that customer's products and search. Tap Exit.
  5. Tap Show everything to undo.

Each customer sees only the products Relisys wants them to see, for example OEM products only for that OEM's customers.

Step 15 · Stop a customer from signing in

  1. Go to Customers, tap a customer, and switch off Allow sign-in. The customer now shows as Blocked.
  2. Tap Sign out (top right) and try to sign in as that customer from the demo list. The app says sign-in is turned off and asks them to contact Relisys.
  3. Sign in as staff again (Step 11) and switch Allow sign-in back on.

Step 16 · Staff access

  1. Tap Staff access in the bottom bar.
  2. You see the SAP users. Superusers show “Always”: they always have access.
  3. Switch on access for another user, for example Manoj Rao (user03).

Step 17 · Sign out

  1. Tap Sign out at the top right.
  2. When you have finished, tap Start over on the prototype page to clear everything.

HelpIf something looks wrong

  • Stuck or confused? Tap Start over, or use the shortcut buttons at the top of the page.
  • Only one phone shows: widen the browser window, or zoom out (Ctrl and minus, or Cmd and minus on a Mac).
  • Things you did disappear: the prototype saves in your browser, so a private window or cleared history starts fresh. This is expected.
  • Page doesn't load: check your internet connection and reload the page.

NextSending your feedback

Please note anything you would change, add or remove. For each point, mention the step number or screen name from this guide so we know exactly where you mean. Screenshots help a lot.

Questions worth thinking about:

  • Are the products, sizes and groups shown the way customers think about them?
  • Is anything missing on the cart or order screens (for example a PO number or remarks)?
  • Are the four order statuses clear enough for customers?
  • Which products should be hidden for which customers?
  • Which Relisys staff should have access to the staff screens?
  • Which notifications should customers get, and by app, SMS or both?
